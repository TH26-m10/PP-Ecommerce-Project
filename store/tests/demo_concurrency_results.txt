======================================================================
  CONCURRENCY TEST 3: Multiple Users Add Same Product to Cart
======================================================================
10 users each add 5 items to cart. Only 20 in stock.

NOTE: Cart does NOT reserve stock — stock is only deducted at checkout.
This test shows that cart_product_create checks current stock at add time,
but concurrent adds can exceed stock since no lock is held between check and checkout.


--- BEFORE ---
  Users:         311
  Products:      308
  ProductOrders: 0
    [OK]  concurrent_cart9 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart1 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart2 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart0 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart8 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart6 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart7 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart3 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart5 added 5
    [OK]  concurrent_cart4 added 5

--- AFTER ---
  Users:         311
  Products:      308
  ProductOrders: 0

>>> RESULTS:
    Success:        10 (expected: 4)
    Failed:         0 (expected: 6)
    Final stock:    20 (expected: 0)
    Total in carts: 50 (expected: 20)

    [INFO] All 10 users added to cart successfully.
    Total in carts: 50 — exceeds stock by design.
    Stock protection happens at CHECKOUT via select_for_update(), not at cart add.
